## The premise

People do not buy products because the features are good. They hire something to make progress in a situation, and they fire whatever they were using before. Demand is created by a struggling moment — a specific, dated, concrete moment of friction — not by a demographic or a persona.

The practical consequence: stop asking customers what they want. Interview people who recently switched and reconstruct what actually happened. Complaints from people who never changed anything tell you little. Only the people who moved can explain why moving was possible.

Expect most of the interview to be noise. The useful part is the portion describing the energy and effort someone spent to make progress.
